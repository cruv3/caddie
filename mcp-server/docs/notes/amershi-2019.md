## TL;DR (my words, after reading)

Consolidates 20+ years of HCI heuristics into 18 validated design guidelines covering initial use, regular interaction, failure ("when wrong"), and learning-over-time. Each guideline is empirically rated for clarity by practitioners. The standard reference for AI UX design heuristics.

## Direct quotes

> "Make clear what the system can do… how well the system can do what it can do… support efficient correction." — paper, Guidelines 1, 2, 9
