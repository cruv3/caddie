## TL;DR (my words, after reading)

Synthesises trust research across psychology, sociology, and human factors into a model of trust *calibration* — the goal is neither maximal nor minimal trust, but trust matched to actual automation capability. Misuse and disuse both follow from miscalibration. The canonical trust-in-automation reference.

## Direct quotes

> "Trust guides reliance when complexity and unanticipated situations make a complete understanding of the automation impractical." — paper
