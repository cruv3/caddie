## TL;DR (my words, after reading)

Foundational critique arguing LLMs are pattern-stitchers without grounded meaning, and that humans systematically over-attribute intent and competence to their outputs. Coined "stochastic parrot" as the framing.

## Direct quotes

> "A system for haphazardly stitching together sequences of linguistic forms... without any reference to meaning." — paper
