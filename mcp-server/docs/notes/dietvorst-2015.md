## TL;DR (my words, after reading)

Five experiments show that once users *see* an algorithm make a mistake — even a small one — they abandon it for an objectively worse human forecaster. Demonstrates that exposure to error is asymmetric for algorithms versus humans.

## Direct quotes

> "People more quickly lose confidence in algorithmic than human forecasters after seeing them make the same mistake." — paper
