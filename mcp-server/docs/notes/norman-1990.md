## TL;DR (my words, after reading)

Diagnoses cockpit-automation incidents and concludes the culprit is not "too much automation" but the absence of continuous feedback that human teams provide each other. Automation at an *intermediate* level of intelligence is the most dangerous — competent enough to act, not communicative enough to be supervised.

## Direct quotes
