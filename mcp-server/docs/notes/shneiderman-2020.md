## TL;DR (my words, after reading)

Rejects the one-dimensional "autonomy slider." Proposes a *two-dimensional* framework where high human control and high automation are not opposites — the best systems maximise *both*. Coins the RST (Reliable, Safe, Trustworthy) goal triple. The reference for "human-centred AI" as a research programme.

## Direct quotes

> "High levels of human control AND high levels of automation… more likely to produce designs that are Reliable, Safe & Trustworthy." — paper
