## Generation brief

All images requested natural smartphone-photography realism, a 4:3 landscape
composition, no logos or watermarks, and no identifiable people. The library,
tram, and collaborative-workspace prompts additionally prohibited readable
text. The whiteboard prompt required only these three German lines:

```text
Projekt
Bericht Dienstag abgeben
Entwurf Donnerstag prüfen
```

The generated files were visually checked for those constraints before being
added to the fixture. They replace the earlier undocumented image set.
